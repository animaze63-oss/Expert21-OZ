# 06 — Staffing Plan

## Guiding principle

Staff for **delivery capacity and client trust**, not for headcount. A mix of senior people, contractors, and AI agents keeps fixed costs low while the firm proves demand. The internal "expert agent team" this repo will build should take over repeatable work (research, proposal drafting, documentation, QA, monitoring) so that human staff spend their time with clients.

## Phased roles

### Phase 1 — Launch (months 0–6): 1–2 people plus contractors
| Role | Type | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Founder / Principal Consultant | Full-time | Sales, discovery, solution design, client relationships |
| AI / Automation Engineer | Co-founder or senior contractor | Builds workflows (n8n, Make, Zapier, Python), LLM integrations, agents |
| Bookkeeper, attorney, designer | Fractional contractors | Back office, contracts, brand |

### Phase 2 — Traction (months 6–18): 4–6 FTE
| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Solutions Consultant / Account Lead (bilingual EN/ES) | Discovery, ROI cases, project management, client training |
| AI Engineer (LLM/agents) | RAG, agents, voice agents, evaluations |
| Automation / Integration Engineer | APIs, CRM/ERP/EHR connectors, data pipelines |
| Delivery / Project Manager | Scoping, SOWs, timelines, retainer reporting |
| Growth / Marketing Coordinator (part-time to full-time) | Content, events, LinkedIn, case studies |

### Phase 3 — Scale (18+ months): 8–12 FTE
Add a Security and Compliance lead (HIPAA, SOC 2), vertical practice leads (real estate, healthcare, legal), a second account executive, and junior automation analysts (possibly from FIU, UM, or Miami Dade College programs).

## Hiring and contractor strategy

- **Use contractors first** for engineering overflow. Convert them to full-time once utilization stays above 70% for one quarter.
- **Bilingual staff are a priority.** Spanish fluency is a selling point in Miami and Latin America.
- **Talent pipelines:** FIU, University of Miami, Miami Dade College (AI programs), eMerge Americas, Refresh Miami, Venture Café Miami, and Built In Miami (about 84 AI companies).
- **Targets:** utilization of 65–75% for engineers and 40–50% for client-facing leads. Revenue per FTE of $180K–$250K.
- **Compensation tools:** commissions for anyone who sells, project bonuses tied to client outcomes, and profit sharing instead of equity in the early years.
- **Required for everyone:** security training, HIPAA training (for the healthcare vertical), background checks for staff with access to client data, NDAs, and IP assignment agreements.
