# 08 — Biggest Risks and Mitigations

Ranked by likelihood × impact for a new Miami AI workflow consultancy.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Commoditization.** Vendors (Microsoft, Google, HubSpot, Salesforce) ship built-in agents, and cheap offshore agencies drive prices down | High | High | Become the *implementation partner* for those platforms. Sell outcomes and governance, not configuration. Build vertical IP and bilingual service. Protect retainer value with monthly KPI reports |
| 2 | **Lumpy pipeline and cash flow.** Long sales cycles, project-heavy revenue | High | High | Paid audits, 50% deposits on builds, push toward retainers (target 50%+ of revenue as recurring by month 12), 4–6 months of cash reserve, net-15 payment terms |
| 3 | **Client data breach or misuse** (PII, PHI, privileged legal data) through AI tools or integrations | Medium | Very high | Least-privilege access, separate credentials per client, enterprise AI tiers with zero data retention, BAAs and DPAs, encryption, logging, incident-response plan, cyber insurance. Meet F.S. 501.171 deadlines: notify clients within 10 days, individuals within 30 |
| 4 | **AI errors or hallucinations cause client harm** (wrong quote, bad legal or medical communication) | Medium | High | Human review on high-stakes outputs, evaluation test sets, confidence thresholds, scope limits in SOWs, **tech E&O insurance** (at least $1M–$2M), limitation-of-liability clauses |
| 5 | **Regulatory change.** Florida AI Bill of Rights (SB 482) likely returns in 2027; federal or state AI rules; Colorado and EU AI Act for multistate or LatAm clients | Medium | Medium | Keep a regulatory watch (assign it to an agent). Design for disclosure, consent, and audit logs from the start so compliance is easy to add |
| 6 | **TCPA/FTSA lawsuits from AI voice or SMS agents** | Medium | High | Prior express written consent workflows, opt-out handling, quiet hours, call recording disclosures. Contractually place consent responsibility on the client, and have counsel review |
| 7 | **Talent cost and retention** (Miami AI salaries $100K–$220K; tight labor market) | High | Medium | Contractor bench (nearshore LatAm), profit sharing, interesting work, training budget, agents doing routine work |
| 8 | **Scope creep and fixed-price overruns** | High | Medium | Paid discovery first, tightly written SOWs, change-order process, reusable templates, a time-tracking review at the midpoint of each project |
| 9 | **Platform and vendor dependency** (API price changes, model retirements, n8n/Make/Zapier term changes) | Medium | Medium | Design so models and vendors can be swapped, avoid hard lock-in, pass usage costs through to clients, include API-change management in retainers |
| 10 | **Key-person risk** (founder-dependent sales and delivery) | High (early) | High | Document playbooks; agent-assisted proposals and delivery; second senior hire by month 9–12 |
| 11 | **Reputation risk from "AI hype" or weak results** | Medium | Medium | Only promise measured outcomes; publish real case studies; pilot before scaling; turn down bad-fit clients |
| 12 | **Local economic shocks** (hurricane season June–Nov, condo and insurance costs, tourism cycles) | Medium | Medium | Cloud-first operations, a remote-capable team, a business continuity plan, clients spread across several verticals and outside South Florida |
| 13 | **IP and ownership disputes** over built workflows and prompts | Low–Medium | Medium | MSAs that separate client-owned deliverables from firm-owned reusable components, licensed to the client |
| 14 | **Florida-specific contracting items** (Cuba-business declaration for the business tax, E-Verify at 25+ employees, workers' comp at 4+ employees) | Low | Low–Medium | Compliance calendar (see `05-permits-and-licenses.md`) |

## Early-warning metrics

- Pipeline coverage below 3× next quarter's revenue target
- Recurring revenue below 30% of total after month 9
- Engineer utilization above 85% (burnout, quality risk) or below 50% (cost risk)
- Any client incident involving data exposure or harmful AI output
- Gross margin on fixed-price builds below 40%

## Must-have contracts and policies before the first client

1. Master Services Agreement (limitation of liability, IP, confidentiality, AI disclaimers)
2. Statement of Work template with acceptance criteria and a change-order process
3. Data Processing Agreement and a HIPAA BAA template
4. Internal AI Acceptable Use and Data Handling Policy
5. Incident Response Plan (mapped to F.S. 501.171 and HIPAA deadlines)
6. Insurance: tech E&O + cyber + general liability (+ workers' comp at 4 or more employees)
