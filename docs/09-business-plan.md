## 1. Executive summary

Experts21-OZ is a bilingual (English/Spanish) AI and workflow-automation consultancy for Miami businesses with 10–250 employees. We find the processes that cost a client the most time, automate them safely within 30 days, and report the results every month.

- **Problem.** 77% of U.S. small and mid-sized businesses now use AI, but 70% of owners say they need more help to use it well. Most have AI subscriptions that are not connected to their real processes, and they worry about data privacy and mistakes.
- **Solution.** A fixed-price ladder of offers: a paid **AI Opportunity Audit**, then a **30-Day Workflow Sprint**, then a **Managed Automation retainer**. Each is built on ready-made playbooks for specific industries, and every project includes data protections and human review.
- **Market.** About 98,000 Miami-Dade businesses with employees. Estimated reachable market: about $12M–$48M a year (`01`).
- **Edge.** Published fixed prices, bilingual delivery, industry playbooks, built-in governance, and monthly outcome reports. No local competitor combines all five (`03`).
- **Financials (base case).** Revenue of **$358K in Year 1, $981K in Year 2, and $1.93M in Year 3**. Pre-tax operating income is $34K, $163K and $487K. Recurring revenue grows from 26% to 52% of the total. Team size grows from 2 to 8.
- **Funding need.** **$75K** of founder capital (Lean startup scenario, about $11.7K of one-time costs). No outside funding is needed in the base case. A downside case shows why hiring has to follow revenue (section 8).

## 3. Services and pricing

**Delivery stack:** OpenAI and Anthropic APIs on enterprise or no-data-retention terms; n8n, Make and Zapier; CRM and practice-management integrations; voice agents (Twilio, Vapi, Retell). We pick the vendor for each client so nothing is locked in, and usage costs are passed through to the client.

## 6. Marketing and sales plan (summary of `07`)

1. **Referral partners:** CPAs, IT providers, CRM resellers, web agencies. Pay a 10–15% referral fee.
2. **Community:** a monthly bilingual "AI for Miami Business" workshop, eMerge Americas, Refresh Miami, chambers of commerce, the realtor association and bar association groups, and the Florida SBDC at FIU.
3. **Founder posts on LinkedIn** (EN/ES), 3–4 per week, showing before-and-after demos.
4. **Search visibility:** pages for each industry and Miami neighborhood, a published pricing page, and listings on Clutch and The AI Agent Index.
5. **Targeted outbound** with short personalized video audits. Email only; no cold texts or calls (TCPA/FTSA).

**Year 1 funnel (base case):** about 2 paid audits and 1 workshop a month. Half of audits turn into builds, and half of builds turn into retainers. That gives about 5 active retainers by the end of Year 1, 14 by the end of Year 2 and 26 by the end of Year 3.

## 8. Financial plan

Base case from `11-financial-model.xlsx` (pre-tax, USD):

| | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total revenue | $358K | $981K | $1.93M |
| — of which retainers | $92K | $419K | $997K |
| Recurring share | 26% | 43% | 52% |
| Gross margin | 74% | 81% | 83% |
| Payroll (with taxes and benefits) | $177K | $494K | $867K |
| Total operating expenses | $232K | $632K | $1.11M |
| **Pre-tax operating income** | **$34K** | **$163K** | **$487K** |
| Net margin | 10% | 17% | 25% |
| Active retainers at year end | 5 | 14 | 26 |
| Headcount at year end | 2 | 6 | 8 |
| Cash at year end (starting with $75K) | $109K | $273K | $759K |

**Downside test.** At half the audit volume (1, 1.5 and 2 a month) with the *same* hiring dates, revenue falls to $220K, $604K and $1.19M. Cash goes negative in Year 2 and bottoms out near –$244K by month 36. **The lesson: tie every hire to the revenue trigger in section 7, not to a calendar date.** With hires tied to triggers, the downside becomes a slower-growth plan rather than a funding crisis.

**Gross margin caution.** Gross margin counts only contractor and platform costs. Staff delivery time sits in payroll. Margin per project should be tracked separately, with a target of at least 50% on fixed-price builds (`08`).

**Use of the $75K starting cash:** about $11.7K for one-time startup costs (the Lean scenario in the model), plus 6 or more months of operating reserve.

## 10. Key risks (summary of `08`)

Price pressure from built-in vendor AI and cheap agencies. Uneven cash flow. Client data breaches. AI errors that harm a client. A Florida AI law (SB 482) likely to return in 2027. Telemarketing-law exposure from AI voice and SMS agents. Talent costs. Dependence on the founder. Each risk has a matching safeguard in `08`. The financial safeguard is hiring tied to revenue triggers plus at least 4 months of cash reserve.
