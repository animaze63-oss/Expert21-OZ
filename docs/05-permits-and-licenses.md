# 05 — Permits, Licenses, and Registrations

> Not legal advice. Requirements and fees change. Confirm each item with the issuing agency, a Florida business attorney, or a CPA. Consulting is not a state-licensed profession in Florida, so there is no "AI consultant license." The requirements below are general business registrations plus duties that come from client data.

## Checklist (in order)

| # | Step | Agency | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check that the name is available, and optionally reserve it | Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) | $25 reservation (optional) | Also check USPTO trademarks and domain availability |
| 2 | **Form a Florida LLC** (Articles of Organization) | Sunbiz | **$125** ($100 filing + $25 registered agent) | Name a registered agent with a Florida street address |
| 3 | Get an **EIN** | IRS | Free | Needed for a bank account, payroll, and the W-9 forms clients will request |
| 4 | Write an operating agreement | Internal / attorney | Attorney fees | Not filed with the state, but banks and partners expect one |
| 5 | Register a fictitious name (DBA), if trading under a different name | Sunbiz | $50 | Requires a newspaper notice first |
| 6 | **City of Miami Certificate of Use (CU)**, if the office is inside City of Miami limits | City of Miami | $50 application fee plus the CU fee | Most businesses need a CU before the BTR. Home-based businesses have special rules |
| 7 | **City Business Tax Receipt (BTR)**, if inside a city (Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral, and others) | That city | Varies by category and headcount | Each city has its own process |
| 8 | **Miami-Dade County Local Business Tax Receipt** | Miami-Dade Tax Collector | Category-based (often about $50–$150 for small offices) | Required for *all* businesses in the county, including those inside cities. Requires the Cuba-business declaration under F.S. 205.0532. Renews by Sept 30 |
| 9 | Florida Department of Revenue registration | FL DOR | Free | For **Reemployment Tax** once you have employees, and for sales tax *only if* you sell taxable goods or services |
| 10 | Federal and state new-hire reporting, I-9, E-Verify | FL New Hire Reporting Center; USCIS | Free | Florida requires **E-Verify** for private employers with **25 or more employees** (F.S. 448.095) |
| 11 | Workers' compensation insurance | Private carrier | See `04` | Florida requires it for non-construction employers with **4 or more employees**, including part-time |
| 12 | Annual report | Sunbiz | **$138.75** | Due Jan 1 – May 1. Late filing costs $538.75 |

## Optional certifications that win business

| Certification | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Miami-Dade County SBE / Micro-Business Enterprise** and **Florida OSD (MBE/WBE/VBE)** | Opens set-aside and preference programs for county and state contracts. Miami-Dade County is actively adopting AI. |
| **Federal SAM.gov registration** (plus SBA 8(a), WOSB, or HUBZone if eligible) | Federal contracting |
| **SOC 2 Type I, then Type II** (Year 2+) | Often required by finance, healthcare, and mid-market buyers. Costs about $20K–$60K with tooling such as Vanta or Drata |
| **ISO/IEC 42001** (AI management system) | An emerging AI governance standard and a premium differentiator |
| Vendor partner programs (Microsoft, Google Cloud, AWS, OpenAI, Anthropic, HubSpot, Zapier, Make, n8n) | Referral leads, credits, and credibility |

## Rules that come with client work (not licenses, but required)

- **HIPAA:** if you touch protected health information for healthcare clients, you are a *business associate*. You must sign a **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)**, follow HIPAA Security Rule safeguards, and use only AI vendors that will sign BAAs.
- **Florida Information Protection Act (F.S. 501.171):** requires reasonable security for personal information. Affected individuals must be notified within **30 days** of a breach, and the Florida Attorney General must be notified if **500 or more** Floridians are affected. Penalties run up to $500K. As a vendor ("third-party agent"), you must notify the client within **10 days**.
- **Florida Digital Bill of Rights (F.S. ch. 501, Part V):** mostly applies to very large companies (more than $1B in revenue), but some of your clients' vendors may pass its requirements down to you in contracts.
- **GLBA / FTC Safeguards Rule:** applies when serving financial institutions, insurance agencies, tax preparers, and auto dealers.
- **TCPA and the Florida Telephone Solicitation Act (FTSA):** AI voice and SMS agents used for outbound contact need prior express written consent. The FCC treats AI-generated voices as "artificial" under the TCPA. The FTSA allows private lawsuits.
- **Florida Bar rules** (Ethics Opinion 24-1 on generative AI): law-firm clients must protect confidentiality and supervise AI output. Build those controls into legal-vertical deliverables.
- **Florida AI legislation (2026):** the proposed AI Bill of Rights (SB 482) **did not pass**. It passed the Senate 35–2 but died in the House on March 13, 2026. HB 1395 and HB 659 also died. SB 484 (ch. 2026-65), which deals with data centers and energy, did become law. Expect the AI Bill of Rights to come back in the 2027 session. Track it (see `08-risks.md`).
