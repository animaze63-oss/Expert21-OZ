# 07 — Marketing and Go-to-Market

## Positioning

**"Miami's bilingual AI workflow firm. We find the processes costing you the most time, automate them safely in 30 days, and report the hours saved every month."**

Proof points to build early: 3 case studies with hard numbers, published pricing, a bilingual website, and a visible governance checklist.

## Productized offer ladder

| Tier | Offer | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | AI readiness quiz plus a 30-minute call; monthly webinar or workshop | $0 | Lead capture |
| Entry | **AI Opportunity Audit**: process map, top 3 automations, ROI, risk notes, roadmap | $2,500–$7,500 (credited toward a build) | Paid discovery; qualifies buyers |
| Training | **Team AI Bootcamp** (EN/ES), half day or full day | $1,500–$5,000 | Fast win; leads to builds |
| Core | **30-Day Workflow Sprint**: one workflow built, tested, and documented | $7,500–$25,000 fixed | Main implementation offer |
| Recurring | **Managed Automation retainer**: monitoring, improvements, monthly KPI report, hour bank | $2,500–$8,000/mo | Predictable revenue |
| Premium | **Fractional Head of AI** | $6,000–$15,000/mo | Mid-market clients |

Vertical packages: "Real Estate Lead Engine," "Clinic Front Desk AI (HIPAA)," "Law Firm Intake Assistant," and "Logistics Quote Bot." Each is pre-built so it can be deployed faster and at a higher margin.

## Channels (ranked by expected ROI for a Miami SMB consulting firm)

1. **Referrals and partnerships** (highest close rate)
   - CPAs, bookkeepers, MSPs/IT providers, CRM resellers, web agencies, and commercial real-estate firms. Offer a **10–15% referral fee** or white-label delivery.
   - Vendor partner directories: HubSpot, Zapier, Make, n8n, Microsoft, Google.
2. **Local community and events**
   - eMerge Americas, Miami Tech Week, Refresh Miami, Venture Café Miami, the Greater Miami Chamber of Commerce, the Miami-Dade Beacon Council, the Hispanic Chamber, the Miami Association of REALTORS, and Dade County Bar sections.
   - Run a **monthly "AI for Miami Business" workshop** in English and Spanish, alternating between in-person and online.
   - Florida SBDC at FIU partnership: co-host training sessions.
3. **LinkedIn founder-led content**
   - 3–4 posts a week: before/after workflow demos, short teardown videos, and ROI numbers. Spanish posts reach LatAm-connected decision makers.
4. **Search and AI-answer visibility (SEO/GEO)**
   - Pages to own: "AI consultant Miami," "AI automation agency Miami," "consultor de inteligencia artificial Miami," plus a page for each vertical and city (Brickell, Doral, Coral Gables, Fort Lauderdale).
   - Publish pricing and FAQ pages. AI assistants and comparison lists (like the Plego and Clutch rankings) pull from these.
   - Google Business Profile, Clutch, and The AI Agent Index listings. Ask clients for reviews.
5. **Targeted outbound** (vertical-specific)
   - Lists by vertical, with a personalized 2-minute Loom audit of each prospect's website or lead flow ("I texted your listing at 9pm and got no reply. Here's what an AI agent would have done.").
   - **Stay compliant:** email under CAN-SPAM; *no* cold texts or calls without consent (TCPA/FTSA).
6. **Paid ads** (test later)
   - Google Search ads on high-intent terms; LinkedIn ads for retargeting. Start at about $1.5K–$3K a month once the landing pages convert.

## Funnel targets (illustrative, Year 1)

| Stage | Monthly target |
|---|---|
| New qualified leads | 30–50 |
| Discovery calls | 15–20 |
| Paid audits or bootcamps | 4–6 |
| Builds started | 2–4 |
| New retainers | 1–3 (target 60%+ of builds converting to a retainer) |

KPIs: customer acquisition cost (CAC) under 15% of first-year contract value; audit-to-build conversion of 50% or more; net revenue retention above 110%; at least one new case study per quarter.

## Messaging by persona

- **Owners:** "Answer every lead in 60 seconds, in English or Spanish, 24/7."
- **COOs:** "Cut X hours a week of re-keying. Pilot price is fixed, and ROI is reported monthly."
- **Law and healthcare:** "Private by design: BAA available, no training on your data, human review built in."

## 90-day launch plan

| Weeks | Actions |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Form the LLC, get licenses, set up contract templates, bilingual website v1, pricing page, LinkedIn profiles |
| 3–6 | Build 2 vertical demos; sign 2–3 discounted "design partner" clients in exchange for case studies |
| 6–10 | First workshop; sign 3 referral partners; publish the first case study |
| 10–13 | Turn design partners into retainers; start outbound in the top vertical |
