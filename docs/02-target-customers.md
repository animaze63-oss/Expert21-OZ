# 02 — Target Customers

## Ideal customer profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Size | 10–250 employees, about $2M–$50M in revenue |
| Location | Miami-Dade first, then Broward and Palm Beach, then LatAm firms with a Miami office |
| Tech maturity | Already uses a CRM, practice-management, or ERP system and some AI seats, but has no in-house AI or automation engineer |
| Pain | Repetitive, high-volume manual work: intake, follow-up, data entry, scheduling, document handling, bilingual customer communication |
| Decision maker | Owner/CEO, COO, managing partner, or operations director |
| Budget signal | Can approve $5K–$25K for a first project and $2K–$8K a month after that |

## Priority verticals (ranked)

| # | Vertical | Why Miami | High-value workflows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Real estate** (brokerages, property management, developers) | One of Miami's largest sectors; high lead volume; bilingual and international buyers | Lead intake and routing, AI follow-up by text and email, listing and marketing content, tenant requests, document checks |
| 2 | **Healthcare practices** (dental, medical spa, specialty clinics, home health) | Large healthcare employment base; heavy scheduling and insurance workload | Scheduling and reminders, insurance pre-verification, patient intake forms, call-center deflection. **Requires HIPAA controls and a BAA.** |
| 3 | **Law firms** (immigration, personal injury, real estate, corporate) | Dense legal market; strong immigration and LatAm practices | Client intake, document drafting support, case-status updates, bilingual communication. Must follow Florida Bar guidance on AI use. |
| 4 | **Logistics, freight, and import/export** | Port of Miami, MIA cargo hub, LatAm trade | Quote generation, shipment-status bots, customs paperwork extraction, email triage |
| 5 | **Hospitality and food service groups** | Tourism; event spikes (World Cup, Art Basel, Miami Music Week) | Reservations and guest messaging, review responses, staff scheduling, inventory |
| 6 | **Financial services, insurance agencies, wealth/family offices** | Citadel effect; growing hedge-fund and family-office base | Client onboarding, KYC document handling, report generation, CRM hygiene. Has strict compliance needs. |
| 7 | **Professional services** (accounting, marketing agencies, architecture) | Many small firms; fast to decide | Proposal and report automation, bookkeeping workflows, internal knowledge assistants |

**Start with 2–3 verticals** (recommended: real estate, healthcare practices, law firms). Each has many local firms, repeatable workflows, and clear ROI. Healthcare and legal also reward a firm that understands compliance.

## Buyer personas

1. **Owner-operator ("Maria, brokerage owner, 35 agents")**: wants more closed deals and less admin. Buys on proof and referrals. Responds to demos and case studies, ideally in Spanish and English.
2. **Operations leader ("David, COO of a 120-person logistics firm")**: wants fewer errors and hours saved. Buys on ROI math and a low-risk pilot. Needs integrations with existing systems.
3. **Managing partner ("Ana, immigration law firm")**: wants intake capacity without new hires. Worried about confidentiality and ethics. Buys on trust and a clear data-handling plan.
4. **Practice manager ("Luis, 3-location dental group")**: wants fewer no-shows and less phone time. Needs HIPAA assurances. Buys on vendor credibility.

## Jobs to be done

- "Answer every lead in under 5 minutes, in the right language, 24/7."
- "Stop my team retyping data between systems."
- "Tell me which AI tools to use and set them up safely."
- "Train my staff so they actually use what we pay for."
- "Make sure we don't leak client data or break a rule."

## Disqualifiers

- Fewer than 5 employees or under $500K revenue (cannot afford it, and the ROI is too small). Point them to a low-cost group workshop instead.
- Wants "AI" with no defined process or owner.
- Regulated data with no willingness to sign a BAA or data agreement, or to follow security controls.
- Expects fixed-price delivery on undefined scope.
