# 03 — Competitive Landscape

## Categories of competition

| Category | Examples (Miami or serving Miami) | Typical rates | Strengths | Weaknesses we can exploit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Big 4 and global consultancies** | PwC (Miami office), Deloitte, Accenture, EY, KPMG | $250–$600/hr; $100K+ projects | Brand, enterprise trust, full stack | Too expensive for SMBs; junior-heavy delivery; slow |
| **Large software and nearshore dev shops** | Neoris, Chetu, BairesDev, Koombea, Appinventiv, 10Pearls, Darwoft, QAT Global | $50–$150/hr blended; $25K–$250K+ | Engineering scale, custom builds | Built around dev projects, not ops workflows; little hands-on change management for SMBs |
| **Boutique AI consulting firms (local)** | Plego, JEH Consulting Services, goHere, Perceptive Analytics (Miami page), Real Estate AI Group (real-estate only), Taycon AI | $125–$300/hr; $10K–$75K pilots | AI focus, senior talent, local presence | Often generalist; few publish prices; limited bilingual focus |
| **SMB managed-IT providers adding AI** | South Florida Tech and other local MSPs | $100–$175/hr or per-seat | Existing client relationships, trust, IT access | AI is an add-on; shallow workflow and LLM skills |
| **AI automation agencies (often remote)** | Many n8n/Make/Zapier agencies; national lists on Clutch, The AI Agent Index | $500–$3,500/mo retainers; $2K–$15K builds | Cheap, fast, productized | Variable quality; no local presence; weak governance |
| **Freelancers and marketplaces** | Upwork, Fiverr AI experts | $35–$150/hr; $200–$8K gigs | Lowest price | No accountability, continuity, or compliance |
| **DIY with vendor tools** | Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, ChatGPT Enterprise, HubSpot/Salesforce AI, Zapier AI | Subscription | Built into tools already in use | Needs internal skill; workflows stay disconnected |

## Local reference points

- **Plego** published a "10 Best AI Consulting Firms in Miami (2026)" list. It puts initial AI readiness assessments at about **$10,000**, and custom ML or private LLM work at **$50K–$250K+**. It names South Florida Tech as the pick for small local retail, legal, and accounting firms.
- **Perceptive Analytics** prices Miami generative-AI pilots at **$25K–$75K**.
- **JEH Consulting Services** markets "governed, closed-loop AI workflow engines" for Miami real estate, fintech, healthcare, and insurance.
- **Real Estate AI Group** is a Miami firm that works *only* with real estate and charges fixed prices for AI agents, workflows, and CRMs. This shows a vertical focus can work locally.
- Built In lists about **84 AI companies in Miami**, which shows the size of the local AI talent and partner pool.

## Positioning gap (our opening)

Few local competitors combine all of the following:

1. **SMB pricing with published, fixed-price packages** (most boutiques hide prices; the Big 4 are out of reach).
2. **Bilingual EN/ES delivery** and LatAm-ready communication.
3. **Vertical playbooks** for 2–3 Miami industries, rather than generic "AI strategy."
4. **Governance built into every project** (data map, AI use policy, human review, logging). This is a selling point for healthcare, legal, and finance.
5. **Outcome reporting** in every retainer (hours saved, response time, error rate, cost per run).

**Suggested positioning statement:** *"Miami's bilingual AI workflow firm. We find the 3 processes costing you the most time, automate them safely in 30 days, and report the hours saved every month."*

## Competitive responses to expect

- MSPs bundling AI "for free" with IT contracts. Answer: show depth and measured outcomes.
- Software vendors (HubSpot, Microsoft, and others) shipping built-in agents. Answer: become the implementation partner for those tools instead of competing with them.
- Offshore agencies undercutting on price. Answer: local presence, accountability, compliance, and Spanish-language support.
