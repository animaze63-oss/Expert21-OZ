# 04 — Startup and Operating Costs

All figures are 2026 estimates in USD. Government fees come from official sources. Everything else is a planning range. Confirm with a CPA before building the financial model.

**Working-capital reserve:** hold **4–6 months of operating expenses** in reserve. Consulting revenue is lumpy, and first deals usually take 30–90 days to close.

## Taxes to plan for

- **Florida has no personal income tax.** A pass-through LLC's profits are taxed federally only.
- **Florida corporate income tax (5.5%)** applies if the entity is a C-corp, or an LLC that elects C-corp taxation.
- **Sales tax:** professional consulting services are generally not taxable in Florida. Reselling taxable items (hardware, some software) can create sales-tax duties. Have a CPA review any SaaS or "information services" resale.
- **Employer payroll taxes:** FICA (7.65%), FUTA, and Florida **Reemployment Tax** (on the first $7,000 of each employee's wages; new employers usually start at 2.7%).
- **Consider an S-corp election** once profit exceeds roughly $80K–$100K, to reduce self-employment tax.

## Revenue model and break-even (illustrative)

| Offer | Price | Gross margin target |
|---|---|---|
| AI Opportunity Audit (2 weeks) | $2,500–$7,500 | 60–70% |
| Workflow build (fixed price, 2–6 weeks) | $7,500–$25,000 | 50–60% |
| Managed automation retainer | $2,500–$8,000/mo | 60–75% |
| Team AI training workshop | $1,500–$5,000/session | 70–80% |
| Fractional "Head of AI" | $6,000–$15,000/mo | 60–70% |

**Standard-model break-even:** about $100K a month in operating expenses ÷ about 60% blended gross margin requires about **$165K a month in revenue**. For example: 20 retainers at an average of $4K ($80K), plus 4 builds a month at $15K ($60K), plus audits and training ($25K).

**Lean-model break-even:** about $10K a month in expenses requires about **3–4 retainers plus one build every other month**.
