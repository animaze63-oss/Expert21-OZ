## Client (anonymized)

An independent residential brokerage in Brickell with 35 agents and 2 admin staff. Its leads come from Zillow, the website and WhatsApp, and about 45% of them prefer Spanish.

## Challenge

- About 600 leads a month.
- Median first response time was **4 hours**, and after 8 p.m. leads waited until the next morning.
- About **30%** of leads never received a follow-up.
- The 2 admin staff spent about **25 hours a week** copying leads into the CRM and assigning them to agents.

## What we built (30-Day Workflow Sprint)

1. A **bilingual AI lead responder** that replies by text and WhatsApp within 60 seconds, asks 3 qualifying questions (budget, area, timeline), and books showings on the agent's calendar.
2. **Automatic CRM entry and round-robin routing** to agents, with a human handoff for anything about pricing, contracts or offers.
3. A **monthly KPI report** covering response time, follow-up rate, showings booked and hours saved.

Compliance: an opt-in consent check, STOP/ALTO opt-out, quiet hours from 9 p.m. to 8 a.m. for non-urgent messages, and no automated cold texting.

## Results after 60 days (fictional)

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Median first response | 4 hours | **under 1 minute** |
| Leads with no follow-up | 30% | **4%** |
| Showings booked per month | 48 | **71** (+48%) |
| Admin hours on lead entry | 25 hrs/week | **6 hrs/week** |

Price: a $12,000 fixed sprint, then a $3,000/month managed retainer.
