## 1. Who we are

**Positioning statement (EN):** Miami's bilingual AI workflow firm. We find the processes costing you the most time, automate them safely in 30 days, and report the hours saved every month.

**Positioning statement (ES):** La firma bilingüe de Miami especializada en automatizar procesos con inteligencia artificial. Identificamos los procesos que más tiempo le cuestan, los automatizamos de forma segura en 30 días y le reportamos cada mes las horas ahorradas.

**Tagline (EN):** Less busywork. More business.
**Tagline (ES):** Menos trabajo repetitivo. Más negocio.

**The five things we always stand for** (every piece of content should reflect at least one):
1. **Bilingual:** English and Spanish, written by people who speak both, not machine translation.
2. **Fixed, published prices:** no surprise invoices.
3. **Industry playbooks:** real estate, healthcare practices and law firms first.
4. **Safe by design:** privacy, human review and compliance built in.
5. **Measured results:** monthly reports on hours saved, response time and errors.

## 3. Voice

We sound like **a trusted local advisor who happens to be an AI expert**: clear, warm, practical and confident, never hyped.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Clear: short sentences, plain words | Jargon-heavy ("leverage synergistic LLM paradigms") |
| Practical: concrete outcomes and numbers | Vague ("transform your business with AI") |
| Warm: friendly, respectful, local | Cold or corporate |
| Confident: we state what we do and what it costs | Boastful or fear-driven ("AI will replace you!") |
| Honest: we say what AI can't do | Over-promising ("100% automated", "guaranteed results") |

## 4. Tone by channel

| Channel | Tone | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Expert and conversational | 120-220 words; a carousel is 6-8 slides | Open with a specific situation or number, not a question like "Are you struggling with…?" |
| Website | Clear and reassuring | Short sections, scannable | Every page shows prices or links to the pricing page |
| Email (outreach) | Personal and respectful | Under 120 words | One clear ask. CAN-SPAM footer always |
| Workshops / slides | Encouraging and hands-on | One idea per slide | Real examples from the audience's own industry |
| WhatsApp / SMS (clients only, with consent) | Friendly and brief | 1-3 lines | Never used for cold outreach |

## 5. Bilingual rules

- **Adapt, don't translate.** Rewrite for meaning and rhythm in each language.
- **Spanish register:** use **usted** for business audiences (owners, partners, managers). Use **tú** only in casual social posts aimed at younger audiences or event recaps, and never in proposals, contracts or the website.
- **Spanish variety:** neutral Latin American Spanish that Cuban, Venezuelan, Colombian and other Miami audiences all read naturally. Avoid regional slang in brand copy.
- **Spanglish:** don't mix languages in a sentence, except for widely used product names (CRM, WhatsApp, software names).
- **Tech terms in Spanish:** "inteligencia artificial (IA)" on first use, then "IA". Use "automatización", "flujo de trabajo" (not "workflow" in Spanish copy), "agente de IA", "recepcionista virtual".
- **Order:** on bilingual pieces, lead with the language of the target audience. Default to EN first on LinkedIn and ES first for Hialeah, Doral and LatAm-focused campaigns.

## 6. Words

**Use:** save hours, answer every lead, fixed price, results in 30 days, safe, private, human review, monthly report, your team, your clients. ES: ahorrar horas, precio fijo, resultados en 30 días, seguro, privado, revisión humana, su equipo.

**Avoid:** revolutionary, game-changer, cutting-edge, disrupt, synergy, leverage, seamless, "unlock the power of AI", "AI-powered everything", "replace your staff". ES: revolucionario, disruptivo, "el poder de la IA", "reemplace a su personal".

## 7. Claims and proof

- Only publish numbers that come from an **approved** case study or testimonial (see `2026-11-23-testimonials-sample.md` for approval status).
- Always say "up to" or give the real range. Never round up. Never present a projection as a result.
- No fake urgency ("only 2 spots left!") unless it's true.
- Never name a client without written approval (MSA section 12.4). Use anonymized descriptions ("a 35-agent Brickell brokerage").
- Don't describe compliance as a guarantee: say "built with HIPAA safeguards and a BAA", not "100% HIPAA compliant".
