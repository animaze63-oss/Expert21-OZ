# Visual Identity: Experts21-OZ (v0.1)

> **SAMPLE STARTER DOCUMENT** created 2026-11-23 for testing. The colors, fonts and logo are placeholders, not a final brand. Replace this file when a designer delivers the real identity.

## Logo

- File: `2026-11-23-logo-wordmark-sample.svg` in this folder (a simple text wordmark).
- **Wordmark:** "Experts21" in Deep Bay navy with "OZ" in Sunrise coral, then a small coral dot.
- **Clear space:** keep empty space around the logo at least as tall as the letter "E".
- **Minimum size:** 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.
- **Don't:** stretch it, recolor it outside the palette, add shadows or effects, or place it on busy photos.
- **On dark backgrounds:** use the white version (swap navy for white and keep the coral).

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Bay (navy) | `#0B2545` | Primary: headlines, logo, buttons |
| Sunrise (coral) | `#FF6B4A` | Accent: highlights, calls to action, the "OZ" |
| Biscayne (teal) | `#13A89E` | Secondary: charts, icons, success states |
| Sand | `#F4EDE4` | Backgrounds |
| Ink | `#1F2933` | Body text |

Contrast: body text is always Ink or Deep Bay on Sand or white. Never use coral for body text, because its contrast is too low.

## Typography

- **Headlines:** Fraunces (Google Fonts), semibold.
- **Body and UI:** Inter (Google Fonts), regular 400 and medium 500.
- Spanish copy runs about 15-25% longer than English, so leave room in layouts.

## Imagery

- Real Miami settings (Brickell offices, Doral warehouses, clinic front desks) and real people at work.
- No robots, glowing brains, blue circuit boards or other AI clichés.
- Diverse, local and professional. Show people using tools, not screens full of code.

## Templates

- **LinkedIn carousel:** 1080×1350 px, Sand background, Deep Bay headline, one idea per slide, with the logo on the last slide only.
- **Before/after slide:** split layout. Left side "Before" in Ink gray, right side "After" with a coral highlight on the metric.
