# SEO and AI-answer visibility (GEO)

Used by: `brand-content-strategist`, `seo-digital-marketer`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Build the keyword and question map: service + Miami/neighborhood (Brickell, Doral, Coral Gables, Fort Lauderdale), industry + AI, Spanish equivalents, and 'how much does it cost' questions.
2. For each target, plan one page: a clear H1, a direct answer near the top, published prices and FAQs (AI assistants quote these), schema markup (Organization, Service, FAQ, LocalBusiness), and internal links.
3. Get listed and reviewed: Google Business Profile, Clutch, The AI Agent Index, partner directories (HubSpot, Zapier, Make, n8n, Microsoft), and Miami 'best AI firm' lists.
4. Track rankings, AI-answer mentions (test the queries in assistants monthly), traffic and leads from organic search.
5. Run paid search only after the landing pages convert (start at about $1.5K-$3K a month).
