# Pricing and packaging

Used by: `competitive-intelligence-analyst`, `cfo-financial-modeler`, `pricing-strategist`, `sales-bd-lead`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Start from the current offer ladder and prices (see reference) and the competitor benchmarks in docs/03.
2. Estimate the delivery cost: hours × loaded cost plus tool and contractor costs. Check the gross margin against the targets.
3. Check value: price as a share of the client's expected Year-1 value (use roi-business-case). Aim for client ROI of 3x or more over a year.
4. Design the packaging: what is included, limits (hours, workflows, revisions), add-ons, the audit credit, and the discount policy (design partners only, time-boxed).
5. Recommend the price, a test plan (for example, quote the new price to the next 5 prospects) and the metric to watch (close rate, margin).

## Reference (from the research in docs/, November 2026; re-verify time-sensitive facts)

- Offer ladder (docs/07): free quiz/call → AI Opportunity Audit $2.5K-$7.5K (credited toward a build) → Team AI Bootcamp $1.5K-$5K → 30-Day Workflow Sprint $7.5K-$25K → Managed Automation retainer $2.5K-$8K/mo → Fractional Head of AI $6K-$15K/mo.
- Gross margin targets (docs/04): audit 60-70%, build 50-60%, retainer 60-75%, training 70-80%, fractional 60-70%.
- Financial-model base prices (docs/11): audit $4K→$6K, build $10K→$15K, retainer $3K→$4K/mo, from Year 1 to Year 3.
