# Partnership development

Used by: `partnerships-community-manager`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Pick the partner types whose clients match the ideal-client profile, and estimate the referral potential of each.
2. Build a target list of specific local firms, with a contact and an angle for each.
3. Design the offer: 10-15% referral fee, white-label delivery, co-hosted workshops, or reciprocal referrals.
4. Write a simple partner agreement outline (have it reviewed by `business-contracts-attorney`): referral definition, fee, payment timing, non-solicit, confidentiality.
5. Onboard each partner with a one-page explainer, an ideal-client cheat sheet and a referral form. Check in monthly and track referrals and revenue by partner.
