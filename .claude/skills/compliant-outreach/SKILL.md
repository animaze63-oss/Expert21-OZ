# Compliant outreach and sales messaging

Used by: `sales-bd-lead`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Pick the channel: email (allowed under CAN-SPAM rules), LinkedIn, warm introduction or event follow-up. No cold SMS or automated calls without prior express written consent.
2. Personalize with the account's trigger. A short Loom 'mini audit' works well ('I messaged your listing at 9pm and got no reply; here's what an AI agent would have done').
3. Write a 3-5 step sequence over 2-3 weeks: value first, one clear ask (a 20-minute call or the free readiness quiz), easy opt-out.
4. Prepare the discovery call: agenda, qualifying questions (volume, pain, systems, budget, decision process), ROI prompts and next step (a paid audit).
5. Prepare objection handling: price, 'we'll do it ourselves with ChatGPT', data privacy, 'tried AI before and it failed'.

## Output template

Sequence (subject + body for each step, EN/ES as needed), a discovery-call script, and an objection-handling table.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] CAN-SPAM: honest subject, physical address, opt-out
- [ ] No non-consented SMS or calls
- [ ] One clear call to action
- [ ] Personalized
