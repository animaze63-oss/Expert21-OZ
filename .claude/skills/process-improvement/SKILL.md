# Process improvement

Used by: `business-process-analyst`, `ai-training-coach`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Plan the discovery: who to interview (owner, operations, front line), what to observe, and which data to request (volumes, times, error logs).
2. Interview with open questions: walk me through it, where does it break, what do you redo, what do customers complain about.
3. Map the current state step by step with time, wait time, hand-offs, systems and pain points.
4. Find waste: re-keying, waiting, rework, unnecessary approvals, after-hours gaps, language barriers.
5. Design the future state: simplify first, then automate. Keep humans on judgment steps.
6. Produce a prioritized opportunity list using roi-business-case.

## Output template

Interview notes, a current-state map, a future-state map (Mermaid or table), and a ranked opportunity list with ROI.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Front-line staff were heard, not just managers
- [ ] Simplify before automating
- [ ] Opportunities ranked by ROI
