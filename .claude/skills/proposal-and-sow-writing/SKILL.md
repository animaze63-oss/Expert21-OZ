# Proposal and SOW writing

Used by: `ai-solutions-architect`, `delivery-lead`, `pricing-strategist`, `sales-bd-lead`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Collect the inputs: client, problem, baseline metrics, the chosen offer, the systems involved, the data types and the deadline.
2. Start from the SOW section of docs/10-msa-and-sow-template.md and fill in every [BRACKET].
3. Write measurable success metrics (baseline → target) and testable acceptance criteria for each deliverable.
4. Fill in the AI-governance block: data types (PHI requires a BAA; personal data requires a DPA), AI providers and their retention settings, human-review steps, prohibited uses, and consent for outbound messaging.
5. Set fees using the standard terms (50% on signing / 50% on acceptance; retainers billed monthly in advance; net-15) and apply the audit credit.
6. List assumptions and out-of-scope items explicitly to prevent scope creep.

## Output template

A completed SOW in the docs/10 format, followed by a short cover note: problem, approach, outcome, price, start date.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] No [BRACKETS] left
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are testable
- [ ] The governance block is complete
- [ ] Fees match the pricing policy
- [ ] Out-of-scope items are listed
