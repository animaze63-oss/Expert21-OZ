# Event and community planning

Used by: `partnerships-community-manager`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Set the goal for each event: leads, partners, brand or hiring, and the target audience.
2. Pick the events: eMerge Americas, Miami Tech Week, Refresh Miami, Venture Café Miami, the Greater Miami Chamber, the Beacon Council, the Hispanic Chamber, the Miami Association of REALTORS, Dade County Bar sections, and the Florida SBDC at FIU.
3. For our own workshops: topic tied to an offer, format (in person or online, alternating EN/ES), venue, registration, run-of-show and follow-up sequence.
4. Budget the event and set targets (attendees, qualified leads, audits booked).
5. Follow up within 48 hours and track the results in the CRM.
