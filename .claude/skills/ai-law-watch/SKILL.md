# AI law and policy watch

Used by: `regulatory-compliance-officer`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Check the current status on official sources: flsenate.gov and myfloridahouse.gov bill pages, the Federal Register, FTC and FCC releases, and state legislature sites.
2. For each item record the bill or rule number, status, effective date, who it applies to and what it requires (disclosures, impact assessments, consumer rights, chatbot or deepfake rules).
3. Assess the impact on (a) the firm and (b) clients in each industry, and the changes needed to templates (docs/10), deliverables or marketing claims.
4. Watch especially for Florida SB 482 (AI Bill of Rights) being refiled for the 2027 session.

## Reference (from the research in docs/, November 2026; re-verify time-sensitive facts)

- 2026 Florida session: SB 482 (AI Bill of Rights; consumer transparency and child protection) passed the Senate 35-2 on 3/4/2026 and died in House messages on 3/13/2026. HB 1395 and HB 659 died. SB 484 (ch. 2026-65), on data centers and large-load utility users, became law.
- FTC Section 5 covers deceptive AI claims, so marketing must not overstate results. The FCC treats AI voices as 'artificial' under the TCPA.
