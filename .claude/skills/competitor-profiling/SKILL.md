# Competitor profiling

Used by: `competitive-intelligence-analyst`, `pricing-strategist`, `brand-content-strategist`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. List the competitors by category: Big 4/global, dev and nearshore shops, local AI boutiques, MSPs adding AI, automation agencies, freelancers, vendor-native AI.
2. For each one, collect from their own site and third-party listings (Clutch, The AI Agent Index, Built In): offers, published prices, target clients, industries, languages, proof points.
3. Score each one against our five differentiators: published SMB pricing, bilingual delivery, industry playbooks, built-in governance, outcome reporting.
4. Write a 'how we win' and a 'where they beat us' line for each.
5. Date every profile and flag what has changed since docs/03.
