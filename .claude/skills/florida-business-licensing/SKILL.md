## Reference (from the research in docs/, November 2026; re-verify time-sensitive facts)

- Sunbiz LLC: $125 ($100 filing + $25 registered agent). Annual report $138.75, due Jan 1-May 1; after May 1 it costs $538.75. DBA $50. Name reservation $25.
- City of Miami: Certificate of Use ($50 application + fee) before the BTR, only inside City limits. Other cities (Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Doral and others) have their own BTRs.
- Miami-Dade Local Business Tax: required for all businesses in the county; amount depends on category and headcount; includes the Cuba-business declaration (F.S. 205.0532); renews by Sept 30.
- EIN (IRS, free). DOR registration for reemployment tax once there are employees; for sales tax only if selling taxable items.
- E-Verify is required for private employers with 25+ employees (F.S. 448.095). Workers' comp is required for non-construction employers with 4+ employees.
