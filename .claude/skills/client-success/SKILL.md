# Client success and retention

Used by: `client-success-manager`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Onboard: kickoff checklist, access, success metrics confirmed, communication rhythm, named contacts.
2. Score health monthly: KPI results vs target, usage, responsiveness, open issues, sponsor engagement, payment timeliness (Green/Amber/Red).
3. For Amber or Red clients, write a recovery plan with an owner and a date.
4. Find expansion opportunities: the next workflow on the audit roadmap, training for new staff, a new location or another department.
5. Ask happy clients for referrals and case studies.
6. Track net revenue retention (target above 110%), churn, and the build-to-retainer conversion rate (target 60%+).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Every client scored monthly
- [ ] Red clients have a plan
- [ ] Expansion tied to delivered value
