# Bilingual content creation

Used by: `voice-conversational-ai-specialist`, `ai-training-coach`, `brand-content-strategist`, `seo-digital-marketer`, `partnerships-community-manager`. Part of the Experts21-OZ team (see `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/expert-roster.md`).

## Procedure

1. Identify the audience, channel, goal and call to action. Choose EN, ES or both. Use neutral Latin American Spanish unless the audience calls for something else.
2. Write in plain language: short sentences, concrete outcomes ('answer every lead in 60 seconds'), no AI hype or unprovable claims.
3. Adapt rather than translate: idioms, examples, formality (usted vs tú for the brand, which defaults to usted for B2B), and the length limits of each channel.
4. Match the positioning: bilingual, fixed prices, results in 30 days, outcomes reported monthly, privacy by design.
5. Check claims against case-study data and the FTC rule against deceptive AI claims. No fabricated testimonials or statistics.

## Output template

Copy in both languages side by side (or the one requested), with the channel, a character count where relevant, and the call to action.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Natural in both languages
- [ ] Claims are true and supportable
- [ ] Consistent with the brand positioning
- [ ] Channel length limits met
